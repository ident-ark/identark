About IdentArk

IdentArk exists between enterprise platforms Indian SMBs cannot operate and leaving identity security unaddressed. We deliver working systems, not slide decks.

Our Story

Built by practitioners, for practitioners

Indian SMBs were either buying enterprise IAM platforms they couldn't operate, or ignoring identity security entirely and waiting for a breach to force the conversation. IdentArk exists between those two failure modes.

Our team has hands-on delivery experience with SailPoint Identity IQ, Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, and Active Directory across BFSI, healthcare, and technology. We deploy, configure, and hand over working systems — not slide decks.

Founded and operating from India, we bring practical awareness of the DPDP Act, RBI IT Framework, and SEBI CSCRF requirements that Indian businesses face.
