About IdentArk

Identity security practitioners passionate about making enterprise-grade IAM accessible to India's growing SMB ecosystem.

Our Story

Built by practitioners, for practitioners

IdentArk was founded by a team of IAM practitioners who spent years implementing identity solutions for enterprises. We saw a pattern: Indian SMBs needed sophisticated identity security, but felt priced out of the market.

So we built IdentArk—purpose-built for India's SMBs, with hands-on expertise in SailPoint, Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, and DPDP compliance. No consultants-by-the-hour. No bloated enterprise pricing. Just honest, expert guidance.

Today, we've helped 100+ SMBs secure their identities, protect 50M+ accounts, and achieve compliance—all without the enterprise overhead.
