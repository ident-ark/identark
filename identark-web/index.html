DPDP Readiness Steps

India's Data Privacy Law
Starts with Identity

Make DPDP requirements operational through clear identity and access controls.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 sets obligations for how organisations collect, process, and store personal data. IdentArk maps identity architecture to purpose limitation, data principal rights, consent management, and breach notification — connecting each requirement to who can access which data and when.

01

Data Minimisation

Scope access to the minimum necessary data and enforce role-based controls at the identity layer.

02

Consent Linkage

Connect identity and access workflows to valid consent context for personal-data processing.

03

Right to Erasure

Use deprovisioning workflows to support consent withdrawal and data-deletion requests.

04

Breach Readiness

Maintain identity audit trails that help establish access history during incident response.
